ADHD Self-Screening Questionnaire

A structured, DSM-5-informed symptom check for teens & adults. One question at a time. Everything runs privately in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Important: this is an educational self-report screen. It cannot diagnose ADHD and cannot prescribe medication. Only a qualified psychiatrist, psychologist, or physician can diagnose ADHD. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency services or a mental-health helpline.
